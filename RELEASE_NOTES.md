# SNUGPT v1.5

**Milestone date · 24 November 2026**

- A redesigned homepage and mobile navigation make it easier to start a chat or browse campus topics. Topic links open an editable draft.
- The chat workspace has clearer campus topics, more room for answers, named source cards, and a shortcut back to the newest message.
- Neon pgvector now powers production retrieval over official admissions and SNU site content, student and undergraduate handbooks, academic policies, cohort-specific CSE and ECE prospectuses, BMS and Economics program material, IT guides, and both CDC newsletters.
- Answers cite the document name and PDF page. Guidance distinguishes archived material and preserves the cohort and scope of policy rules.
- Answer reporting, browser history export, print to PDF, and calendar event downloads are available in chat.
- Google and GitHub buttons display an animated maintenance notice that respects reduced-motion settings.
- The paper plane game responds faster and scrolls obstacles at a brisker pace.
- About, Privacy, Terms, and License reflect the current product and source coverage.
